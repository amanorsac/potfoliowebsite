# Blog pictures

Pictures for posts go here. It sits under `images/` with everything else,
so a picture in this folder is reachable at:

    /images/blog/whatever-you-called-it.webp

which is what goes in the post.

## There are two ways to get a picture into a post

**Drag it into the editor.** Portal → Writing → open a post → drag the
picture into the writing box. It uploads itself and drops in the line
that displays it. Nothing to do with this folder, nothing to commit. This
is the easy way and the right one for most posts.

**Put it here.** Better when you are handing me a post to prepare: send
me the pictures, I put them in this folder alongside the writing, and the
markdown I hand back already points at them. You paste and publish
without hunting for anything.

## Sending me a post

Send the writing and the pictures together and say which picture goes
where — "this one after the second paragraph" is enough. What I hand back
is a block of text ready to paste, with:

- the title and the one-line summary that becomes the blog card and the
  line under the title in Google
- the topic, matching one of the chips on the blog page
- the body, with the pictures already placed and described
- the header image, if one of them suits

**I cannot publish it for you.** Writing to the posts table needs an
admin sign-in, and the only key I have is the public one that can just
read published posts. That is deliberate — it is the same rule that stops
anyone else writing to your blog. So the last step is yours: paste,
look at the preview, press Publish. About a minute.

## Naming

Lower case, hyphens, no spaces, and say what it is rather than what the
camera called it:

    rehearsal-chart-on-stand.jpg      not   IMG_4021.jpg
    console-during-mix.jpg
    choir-sectional.jpg

## What I do with them

Resize to what the page actually displays, convert to WebP, and write the
description that goes with each one — the line a blind reader hears and
Google reads. Send the originals at full size; they get smaller here, and
starting from something already squashed only makes them worse.
