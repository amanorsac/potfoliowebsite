# Gallery pictures

Photographs for the gallery on the About page go here. The folder sits
under `images/` with everything else, so a picture put here is reachable
at:

    /images/gallery/whatever-you-called-it.webp

## How to add one

Two steps, and only the first is yours.

**1. Put the file in this folder.** Any name that says what it is —
`accra-session-2024.jpg`, `sunday-broadcast.jpg`. Lower case, hyphens
instead of spaces, no apostrophes. Commit it.

**2. Tell me it is there, and what it is a picture of.** One line each:

    accra-session-2024.jpg — the band tracking at the Accra studio

That second half is not decoration. It becomes the caption under the
picture and the description a blind visitor's screen reader reads out,
and it is the only part I cannot write for you, because I was not there.

Then I add the picture to the list on the About page and convert it —
see below for why that is worth doing rather than skipping.

## What to send

**Full size, straight off the camera or phone.** Do not shrink anything
first. A 4000px photograph converts down cleanly; a 900px one that has
already been through WhatsApp cannot be rescued, and it shows on a big
screen.

JPEG or PNG or HEIC are all fine. So is a folder of them.

## What happens to them

Every photograph on this site is converted to WebP at the size it is
actually displayed. That is why the pages load as fast as they do: the
gallery is the heaviest thing on the About page, and a wall of unconverted
phone photographs would be twenty megabytes of it.

The gallery is also built to stay quick as it grows — the first twelve
are in the page, the rest arrive when someone asks for them — so there
is no number at which adding another becomes a problem. Send fifty if
you have fifty.

The originals are kept alongside the converted files, so nothing is lost
and a picture can be redone at a different size later.

## What is already in the gallery

Four, listed on the About page: writing at the keys in Accra, directing
from the keys, the live set, and the band at TV3 Ghana. The first one in
the list is the big one across the top, so put the strongest picture

## One thing worth knowing

A gallery of photographs of the same room reads as a smaller gallery than
it is. Variety carries it: the desk, a stage, hands on keys, a full band,
a control room, somebody laughing between takes. Four good and different
beats twelve of the same evening.
