HarmonieMDSoprano · Alto · Tenor · Song

01 · Rehearsal studio

Walk in knowing your part

A rehearsal and multi-track recording studio built for worship teams and choirs. The Music Director records or uploads a song with every vocal harmony part; each singer opens it at home, solos their own line, slows it down, and loops the bridge until it locks.

It speaks choir natively — not tracks 1–8 but Soprano, Alto, Tenor, each with two lanes. Choir Mode is a four-fader practice tool a volunteer learns in ten seconds; MD Mode is a genuine multi-track editor with take comping underneath.

  • Per-part solo & faders
  • Slow down without pitch change
  • Measured round-trip latency
  • “Mark practiced” visibility
  • Local-first — works offline
  • Explore: share arrangements

PerformLive4 decks · stems · scenes

02 · Live performance

Play the stems, not the bounce

A stem player and loop performance instrument for the stage. Load your songs’ stems — drums, bass, keys, vocal, pads — onto a grid of decks, then trigger, mute, layer, and switch in real time. Drop the drums for a breakdown. Hold a pad under a prayer. Jump the set order without fumbling.

Your audio is never touched — no auto-warp, no normalising; the mix your producer built is what the room hears. And it’s built touch-first, so a volunteer can run a service after fifteen minutes.

  • Per-stem mute & layers
  • Bar-quantised switching
  • Scene recall in one tap
  • MIDI Learn everywhere
  • Untouched-audio principle
  • Touch-first targets

Nebula Tide12 keys · zero pitch-shifting

03 · Ambient instrument

An ocean of sound, in every key

Endless, seamless drone pads inside an interface that’s alive. Every preset is a real recording captured in all twelve keys — never pitch-shifted — so E♭ sounds as intentional as C. Keys orbit as floating planets; tap one and the pad crossfades into a new tonal world.

Two stars flank the stage — FX and textures like rain, wind, and swells — and Room, Plate, and Hall reverbs shape the space. Runs standalone on Windows or as a VST3 in your DAW, and the whole interface re-colors itself to match the sound.

  • Seamless infinite looping
  • 12 real recordings per preset
  • FX & texture layers
  • Room / Plate / Hall
  • Standalone + VST3
  • Preset packs, drop-in

PulseRoomDelay · Reverb · EQ · Comp

04 · Producer toolkit

Every mixing answer. One tempo.

The reference desk beside your DAW. Tap in your song’s BPM once and everything follows: tempo-synced delay times, reverb pre-delay and decay that land on the bar grid, EQ boost-and-cut zones painted for 13 instruments, and compression you can finally see as a drawn gain-reduction envelope.

Every number ships with its why, and the app’s own footer says the rest: values are starting points — trust your ears first. Offline, no account, no ads.

  • Tap-tempo drives it all
  • Click-to-copy every value
  • Visual EQ & compression
  • Mix chains with reasons
  • LUFS & reference tables
  • Free · Windows & macOS
