Questions, answered.

Do I need musical training to use it?
No. If you can move a volume slider, you can use Choir Mode.
Does my whole team need to pay?
No. One subscription covers your whole team. Members join free with your code.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Songs download to the device once and play without a connection. You only need internet to get new songs or share recordings.
What do I need to record?
A laptop and any microphone, including the built-in one. Run the one-time sync calibration first and takes will land in time. For best results use wired headphones; Bluetooth adds large and variable delay.
Can I use my audio interface?
Yes. Input and output devices are selectable in the app.
What if I re-record and the first take was better?
Every take is kept. Switch back whenever you like.
Is my music private?
Yes. Songs are private to your team by default. Only songs you explicitly publish appear on Explore.
Which platforms?
Windows desktop today.
